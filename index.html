03About

It started with software.

I've been building software for more than 15 years, so I already knew how I wanted the work done. But AI would often start building too early, make too many decisions at once, or produce changes that were difficult to review.

Instead of correcting the same problems every time, I wrote down my process: plan before coding, discuss difficult decisions one at a time, and keep every change reviewable. The more clearly I showed AI how I worked, the more useful it became.

Then I noticed the same opportunity elsewhere: preparing monthly invoices, planning and scheduling social media content, and keeping track of other tasks that repeatedly come back.

Now I'm exploring how far this idea can go—first with my own work, then with independent professionals and small-business owners. I want to find real recurring problems, see whether AI can make one part easier, and share honestly what works and what doesn't.

Things I'm teaching my AI

I'm starting with problems I know well. Some already help me today; others are experiments I'm still building.

  1. 01

    Preparing my monthly invoices

    Every month, I convert payments from USD to EUR, enter everything into Excel, and prepare the files for my bookkeeper. I'm teaching AI to handle those repeated steps with me.

    Building now
  2. 02

    Managing my social media content

    I use AI to develop ideas, plan and schedule posts, and keep track of what I've already published—so I don't have to rebuild the process each time.

    Using now
  3. 03

    Building software my way

    I've taught AI to plan before coding, discuss difficult decisions one at a time, and keep changes small enough to review.

    Using now

These are my own recurring problems. Next, I want to meet people with completely different work, understand what they keep doing manually, and see whether AI can make one part easier.
